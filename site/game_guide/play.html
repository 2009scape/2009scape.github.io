To get started, all you need to do is:

  1. Download Saradomin Launcher:
    Windows
    Linux (Direct binary)
    Mac
  2. Run the launcher
  3. Open the settings tab and tune the settings as you please, but make sure you don't change the server profile. It should be set to stable server.
  4. Click play! The client and Java 11 will update/download as necessary.
  5. Once the client has loaded, create an account from the main menu. If you get stuck on a "please wait" message, restart the client and try again - this happens sometimes if you have a fast connection.
  6. Log in and play!

About

Welcome to 2009scape, where your memories of RuneScape come to life. Unlike Old School RuneScape, we keep true to the Gower brothers' vision of the game.

This project is a constant work in progress. The goal is content completion up until January 2009 (revision 530), with an expansion to December 2009 (revision 578) planned. We encourage you to discuss any suspected bugs you find on the forum or Gitlab.<\a> You can read more on our about page.

While we strive for authenticity, we have made a few quality-of-life (QoL) improvements along the way. We do not accept donations, or any form of financial contribution. If you wish to contribute you can help maintain the wiki, become a tester, fix issues, write new content, or help us maintain our drop tables, item config, etc.

2009scape consists of three main components:

  • Server - where the game runs, hosted by volunteers
  • Client - where the game is displayed and interacted with
  • Launcher - updates, configures and runs the game client

You may request your live server save file at any time.

If you have any questions that are not answered here, please ask on the forums.

Tutorial Island

The famous tutorial island has been added to 2009scape! Once you have created your account, you will spawn at the start of tutorial island. You can skip the tutorial by talking to Skippie.

During the tutorial, everyone plays at 1x experience. You will be given the option to set your experience rate and game mode at the end of the tutorial by speaking to the wizard.

Changing Ironman Modes

  • Ironman Mode

    • This account mode is the basic of the three Ironman account types. Ironmen are unable to trade any other player, so they are completely self-sufficient. They also cannot use lootshare whatsoever, which means they are still stuck collecting everything on their own.

  • Ultimate Ironman

    • In addition to regular ironman mode restrictions, Ultimate Ironmen cannot bank items. They can still note and unnote items by using them on bank booths.

  • Hardcore Ironman (Permadeath)

    • Permadeath Hardcore Ironman was a limited account mode and is no longer available for new characters.


Starter Items

Bronze Axe - The bronze axe can be found in the chicken coup across the bridge of Lumbridge across from the cows. It is sticking in a tree stump for you to grab.
Bronze Pickaxe - The bronze pickaxe can be found by going up the ladder in the gate house in Lumbridge castle. It is located on the third floor.
Tinderbox - The tinderbox can be found in the Draynor Manor on the third floor near the professor you speak to during the Ernest the chicken quest (can also be bought from the general store for a few coins).
Small Fishing Net - The small fishing net can be found near the fishing tutor in Lumbridge swamp all over the ground.
Knife - A knife can be found on top of the crate in the Lumbridge Castle kitchen basement or behind Bob's Axe Shop.
Iron Dagger - You can find an iron dagger in the Goblin house across the bridge in Lumbridge.


Early Money

RuneScape offered plenty of different ways to make money. It can be difficult hopping into the time machine and coming back to this era. Some basic money making methods are below, with further information and tips on the wiki.

Tip: The Bandit Camp's General store buys items for high alch value if you are unable to find players to trade or do not have the magic level

Jobs

Jobs are repeatable tasks given by skill tutors that introduce you to each skill while providing useful rewards. Speak with a tutor and ask for a job. They give you a task to complete which generally ties to their assigned skill. You can accept 3 jobs a day. You are able to cancel a job by asking the tutor for a new task.

Shooting Stars

Shooting Stars are a community event where people work together to break down the star for rewards. They occur roughly every five and a half hours. Once per day, you can exchange 200 stardust for cosmic runes, astral runes, gold ore, and 50,000 coins.


Pyramid Plunder

This thieving minigame is an excellent early-game moneymaker, gives good experience, and only requires level 21 thieving to get started. Reward items can be traded for coins, and the minigame has a chance of rewarding you with the Pharaoh's Sceptre, which provides useful teleports.

Agility Pyramid

You will need a way to make sure you don't die of the desert heat and a minimum of level 30 agility to get started. Return the Pyramid Top to Simon to receive between 3,700 and 10,000 coins, depending on your Agility level. After grabbing the pyramid top, remember to exit the course via the top exit to reset the reward instead of teleporting away. Otherwise, you will not be able to obtain another Pyramid Top until completing the course again and using the exit.

Stronghold of Security

Make your way over to the stronghold of security underneath the Barbarian village. Progress through the maze while answering the player safety questions. Completing the Stronghold rewards you with 10,000 coins and a pair of Fighting Boots or Fancy Boots.



Fast Traveling

Gnome Gliders

Completing The Grand Tree quest will give you access to Gnome Gliders, a form of fast travel connecting several locations.



Spirit Trees

You may travel from any Spirit Tree directly to any other Spirit Tree in the game after completing the Tree Gnome Village quest. You can use Spirit Trees to travel to the Tree Gnome Stronghold after the Tree Gnome Village quest, but need to complete the Tree Gnome Stronghold quest to use the Spirit Tree there.


Canoes

One travel method a lot of people forget about are the canoes!


Fairy Rings

Fairy Rings are currently available after completing Lost City, rather than the Fairy Tale quest series.



Extra

Quests

Quests are a major part of progression in 2009Scape. While not every quest has been implemented yet, new quests are added regularly, and the plan is to add all the quests eventually in the game. When a quest is not implemented, you can access certain areas or rewards associated with them by completing the associated requirements. You can find all the quests inside the website under quest help. The quest cape works as well. When new quests are added, you will no longer be able to wear the cape until that quest is completed.


Achievement Diaries

Diaries have been added to the game, and certain content is locked behind these achievements. Completing the Lumbridge/Draynor diaries will give you an explorer ring, which offers unlimited teleports you to the Falador cabbage patch after compl There are plenty of other perks of completing the diaries! So click the little green star in your quests tab to check them out! Currently, all tiers of the Lumbridge and Seer's Village Diary, and the first tier of all other diaries.


Dailies

Daily activities provide additional rewards without being required for progression. Examples include Shooting Stars, Penguin Hide and Seek, and Battlestaves. Guides for these activities can be found on the wiki. You can find guides to these on our local Wiki.
